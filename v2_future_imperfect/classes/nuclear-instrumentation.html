Nuclear Instrumentation (ENU840)

Postgraduate Discipline | PCTN / UFMG

PCTN Program Prof. Telma Fonseca

Syllabus Overview

Radiation sources and interaction mechanisms. Counting statistics, error propagation, and dead-time corrections. Gas-filled detectors (ionization chambers, proportional counters, Geiger-Müller counters), scintillation detectors (NaI(Tl), plastic, liquid), photomultiplier tubes, semiconductor detectors (HPGe, Si(Li), CZT), multichannel spectrometry, and nuclear pulse processing electronics.
