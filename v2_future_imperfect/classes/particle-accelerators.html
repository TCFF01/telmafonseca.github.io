Particle Accelerators (ENU856)

Postgraduate Discipline | PCTN / UFMG

PCTN Program Prof. Telma Fonseca

Syllabus Overview

Physics and engineering of charged particle acceleration. Electrostatic accelerators (Cockcroft-Walton, Van de Graaff), Betatrons, Microtrons, Cyclotrons, Synchrotrons, and Linear Accelerators (LINACs). RF cavities, microwave power systems, beam transport optics, magnetic focusing lattices, particle beam diagnostics, and clinical/industrial accelerator applications.
