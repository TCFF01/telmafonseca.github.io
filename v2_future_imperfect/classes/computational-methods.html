Computational Methods (ENU874)

Postgraduate Discipline | PCTN / UFMG

PCTN Program Prof. Telma Fonseca

Syllabus Overview

Neutral particle transport theory, monoenergetic and multigroup transport equations. Finite difference and finite element discretizations in 1D, 2D, and 3D geometries. Stochastic Monte Carlo simulation methods, variance reduction techniques, GPU acceleration, computational phantoms, and MCMEG open-education Monte Carlo course integration.
