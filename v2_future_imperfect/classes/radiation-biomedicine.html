Radiation Applied to Biomedicine (ENU887)

Postgraduate Course | PCTN / UFMG | Semester 2026-2

Semester 2026-2 Prof. Telma Fonseca

Course Overview

Physical and biological basis of radiation applications in medicine. Unsealed radioactive sources for internal radionuclide therapy, radioimmunotherapy, high-dose-rate (HDR) and low-dose-rate (LDR) brachytherapy, teletherapy (medical LINACs, Cobalt-60), Boron Neutron Capture Therapy (BNCT), proton and heavy-ion therapy, and radiopharmaceutical treatment planning.
