Radiobiology (ENU883)

Postgraduate Course | PCTN / UFMG | Semester 2026-2

Semester 2026-2 Prof. Telma Fonseca

Course Syllabus & Objectives

Physical, chemical, and biological mechanisms of ionizing radiation interaction with biological tissue. DNA damage repair, chromosome aberrations, cell survival curves, linear energy transfer (LET), relative biological effectiveness (RBE), oxygen enhancement ratio (OER), tissue kinetics, acute radiation syndrome (ARS), late somatic and hereditary effects, and radiobiological principles of radiotherapy fractionation.

Key Reference Material

Includes recommended IAEA Training Course Series TCS-42 (Radiation Biology: A Handbook for Teachers and Students) and international radiation protection guidelines.
