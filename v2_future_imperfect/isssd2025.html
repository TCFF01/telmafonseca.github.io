ISSSD 2025 - XXV International Symposium on Solid State Dosimetry

Belo Horizonte, MG, Brazil | September 22–26, 2025

Sept 22–26, 2025 CDTN/CNEN

The XXV International Symposium on Solid State Dosimetry (ISSSD 2025) is organized by the Centro de Desenvolvimento da Tecnologia Nuclear (CDTN/CNEN) in Belo Horizonte, MG, Brazil.

The symposium gathers researchers, medical physicists, engineers, and international experts to discuss the latest advancements in solid-state radiation detectors, luminescent dosimeters (TLD, OSL), EPR dosimetry, nanodosimetry, microdosimetry, and radiation protection applications.

Prof. Telma Fonseca is a member of the organizing/scientific committee for ISSSD 2025.
