DEN / PCTN - UFMG Prof. Telma Fonseca

Associate Professor at the Department of Nuclear Engineering (DEN), Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG). She teaches in the Graduate Program in Nuclear Science and Techniques (PCTN/UFMG), in the field of Radiation Sciences, and collaborates with the Graduate Program in Physics at UFMG and the Centro de Desenvolvimento da Tecnologia Nuclear (CDTN/CNEN).

She is an Editorial Board Member of the international journal Applied Radiation and Isotopes (ARI) and Secretary of the Management, Diversity and Inclusion Committee of WinBrasil – Women in Nuclear (2024–2027). She is also the founder and coordinator of the Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group (MCMEG).

She holds a degree in Computational Sciences, a Master's degree in Nuclear Science and Techniques (Radiation Sciences) from UFMG, and a Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences from Katholieke Universiteit Leuven (KU Leuven), Belgium. Between 2008 and 2014, she was a collaborating researcher at the Studiecentrum voor Kernenergie (SCK-CEN), Mol, Belgium, and head of the Internal Dosimetry Laboratory (LDI) at CDTN/CNEN (2014–2017). In 2015, she received the International PROCORAD Award in radiotoxicology.

Her expertise spans computational modeling, GPU-accelerated Monte Carlo simulation, artificial intelligence, micro- and macrodosimetry, radiotherapy, radiobiology, and advanced radiation detectors.

Computational Experiments in Radiation Physics

Written by Lucas Paixão and Telma Fonseca

Academic Book Amazon Publication

This book presents nine computer-based experiments built around the Monte Carlo method, designed to complement nuclear instrumentation and radiation detection education. It aims to enrich the didactic material used in teaching and research by pairing computational models with traditional laboratory practices in Medical Physics, Radiologic Technology, and Nuclear Engineering courses.

The nine experiments:

  • Radioactive emission spectra
  • Count statistics
  • Dosimetric quantities
  • Inverse square law
  • Photon attenuation
  • Scattering
  • Absorbed dose
  • Charged particle interaction
  • Neutron source simulation

The book assumes basic computational skills, familiarity with spreadsheet applications, and introductory experience with the Monte Carlo code used in the experiments.

Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group (MCMEG)

Computational Simulation & Open Science

Research Group MCMEG

The Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group (MCMEG) is an international multidisciplinary research group coordinated by Prof. Telma Fonseca. MCMEG focuses on high-performance computational transport simulations, GPU acceleration, phantom development, and Monte Carlo applications in medical physics and nuclear technology.
