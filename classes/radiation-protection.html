Radiation Protection

Course Code: ENU827
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

  • Ionizing Radiation & Matter Interaction: Fundamental concepts of ionizing radiation and radiation interaction with matter.
  • Dosimetry & Microdosimetry: Definition of dosimetric quantities, exposure and dose calculations; X-ray, Gamma, Beta, and Neutron dosimetry; microdosimetry.
  • Radiological Protection & Biological Effects: Biological effects of radiation, maximum permissible dose limits, and radiation protection principles.
  • Shielding & Risk Assessment: Shielding design calculations, recognition, evaluation, and control of occupational and environmental risks.
  • Operational Principles & Legislation: Fundamental operational principles, basic legislation, regulatory standards, and radiation detectors for radiation protection.

  • Radiological Health Handbook. U.S. Department of Health, Education, and Welfare.
  • Price, W. J. Nuclear Radiation Detection. McGraw-Hill.
  • Attix, F. H. & Roesch, W. C. Radiation Dosimetry. Academic Press.
  • Technical Publications: ICRP, NCRP, ICRU, IAEA Safety Series, NBS.
