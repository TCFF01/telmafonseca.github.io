Teaching Internship

Course Name: Supervised Teaching Internship (Estágio Docente)
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Overview

Pedagogical practice in higher education under faculty supervision, integrating instructional planning, classroom teaching, and learning assessment with reflective analysis of academic routines.

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

  • Instructional Planning: Preparation of lesson plans, course schedules, and development of instructional materials tailored to undergraduate course levels.
  • Supervised Teaching Practice: Supervised delivery of theoretical lectures, laboratory practical sessions, or directed study activities.
  • Assessment & Student Mentoring: Formulation and grading of assessment instruments, combined with academic tutoring and student support.
  • Critical Reflection: Critical analysis of classroom methodological challenges and the professional development process in higher education teaching.
