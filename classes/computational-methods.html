Computational Methods

Course Code: ENU874
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

  • Analytical & Variational Methods: Review of special solutions of linear and non-linear partial differential equations by analytical methods: integral transform method; variational method.
  • Particle & Neutron Transport Theory: Application to neutral particle transport equations; concepts of nodal methods; numerical methods applied to neutron transport.
  • Finite Difference Methods: Finite difference methods applied to monoenergetic and multigroup diffusion equations.
  • Finite Element Methods: Finite element methods applied to transport equations; 2D and 3D space discretization.
  • Mathematical Formulations & 2D/3D Modeling: Mathematical development of the finite element technique applied to two-dimensional and three-dimensional media for transport equations.
