Nuclear Instrumentation

Course Code: ENU840
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

  • Radiation Sources & Interactions: Radiation sources, radiation interaction mechanisms with matter, general detector properties.
  • Statistics of Counting: Counting statistics, deviation prediction, and error propagation.
  • Gas-Filled Detectors: Ionization chambers, proportional counters, and Geiger-Müller (GM) counters.
  • Scintillation & Semiconductor Detectors: Scintillation detectors, photomultiplier tubes (PMT), scintillator spectrometry, semiconductor diode detectors, Ge(Li) and solid-state detectors.
  • Neutron Detection: Thermal and fast neutron detection methods.
  • Electronic Signal Processing: Pulse shaping, linear and logic functions, Multichannel Analyzers (MCA).
  • Shielding & Background: Special radiation detectors, shielding calculations, and background radiation counting.

Recommended Bibliography

  • Knoll, Glenn F. Radiation Detection and Measurement. John Wiley & Sons, 1979.
  • Price, W. J. Nuclear Radiation Detection. McGraw-Hill, 1964.
