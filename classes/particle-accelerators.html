Particle Accelerators

Course Code: ENU856
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

  • Accelerator Overview & Particle Dynamics: Needs, historical context, and accelerator-based radiotherapy modalities; review of particle dynamics, electric/magnetic forces and fields, electric/magnetic lens systems, and particle orbits in focal fields.
  • Types & Characteristics of Accelerators: High-voltage pulsed electrostatic accelerators, Betatrons, resonant cavities and waveguides, Microtrons, Cyclotrons, Synchrotrons, Linear Accelerators (LINACs), and RF linear accelerator fundamentals.
  • Microwave Systems & Beam Optics: Microwave accelerator structures, microwave power sources, pulse modulators, auxiliary systems, beam optical/magnetic systems, and X-ray generator accelerators.
  • Nuclear Codes & Advanced Modeling: Calculation codes and nuclear codes applied to particle accelerators; novel acceleration source models and techniques.
  • Dosimetry & Applied Radiobiology: Dosimetry measurement methods and radiobiology principles applied to particle accelerators.
  • Applications & Clinical Experience: Synchrotron radiation applications, radioisotope production via accelerators, clinical cancer therapy using fast, epithermal, thermal neutrons, charged particles, and mesonic beams.

Recommended Bibliography

  • Krane, Kenneth S. Introductory Nuclear Physics. John Wiley & Sons, 1988.
  • Scharf, W. H. Biomedical Particle Accelerators. AIP Press, 1993.
