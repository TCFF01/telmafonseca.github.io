Course Syllabus & Main Topics

1. Physical, Chemical, and Biological Aspects of Ionizing Radiation

  • Basic concepts, ionizing radiation types, and direct vs. indirect biological actions of radiation.
  • Radiation physics and chemistry concepts; chromosomal aberrations, DNA single- and double-strand breaks.

2. Radiobiology & Cellular Response

  • Cell survival curves and mathematical models; dose-response relationships for normal tissues; tumor modeling systems.
  • Radiosensitivity, mitotic cycle dynamics, radiation damage repair, dose-rate effects, oxygen effect, and tissue reoxygenation.
  • Linear Energy Transfer (LET) and Relative Biological Effectiveness (RBE); radiosensitizers and radioprotectors; tumor, tissue, and cell kinetics.

3. Radiotherapeutic Modalities & Clinical Effects

  • Overview of current clinical modalities utilizing ionizing radiation in therapy.
  • Time, dose, fractionation relationships in clinical radiotherapy; emerging radiotherapy modalities; hyperthermia applications.
  • Acute radiation effects, radiation carcinogenesis, hereditary radiation effects, radiation effects on embryos/fetuses, and molecular techniques in radiobiology.

4. Radioisotope Therapy & Targeted Applications

  • Diagnosis: Technology and clinical techniques in nuclear medicine.
  • Systemic Radiotherapy: Fundamental concepts and dosimetry.
  • Targeted Applications: Radioiodine therapy, radioisotopic synovectomy, treatment of bone pain/metastases, radioimmunotherapy (antibody-guided therapy), and radioguided surgery.

5. Concepts in Brachytherapy

  • Physical principles, radioisotopes, and sealed sources in brachytherapy.
  • Equipment, apparatus, computational dosimetry, High-Dose-Rate (HDR) and Low-Dose-Rate (LDR) techniques, and endovascular brachytherapy.

6. Photon and Electron Teletherapy

  • Physical fundamentals, equipment, and apparatus.
  • Fundamentals in megavoltage teletherapy, photon beam dosimetry, radiotherapy treatment planning (RTP), electron beam therapy, and stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS).

7. Fast Neutron Radiotherapy & High-LET Modalities

  • Fast neutron teletherapy and fast neutron brachytherapy.
  • Boron Neutron Capture Therapy (BNCT), Californium-252 (252Cf) brachytherapy, and coupling with NCT.

8. Bases of Charged Particle Radiotherapy & Risk Evaluation

  • Physical and biological fundamentals in proton therapy.
  • Radiology and operational practices with ionizing radiation: risk evaluation versus clinical benefits.
