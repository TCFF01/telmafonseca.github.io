Medical Imaging

Course Code: ENU860
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

  • History & Fundamentals: History of medical imaging research; image theory and reconstruction from projections.
  • X-ray & Transmission CT: X-ray imaging, transmission computed tomography (CT), charged particle radiography and tomography.
  • Emission Tomography & Nuclear Medicine: Radionuclide imaging, gamma emission tomography (SPECT), and Positron Emission Tomography (PET).
  • Ultrasound & Tissue Analysis: Ultrasound imaging and tissue texture analysis.
  • Impedance & Thermal Imaging: Tissue impedance measurements, image formation, thermal imaging, and diaphanography.
  • Magnetic Resonance Imaging: Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI).

Recommended Bibliography

  • Herman, G. T. Image Reconstruction from Projections: The Fundamentals of Computerized Tomography. Academic Press, 1980.
  • IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science, IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, Journal of Nuclear Medicine.
  • Jackson, D. F. Imaging with Non-Ionizing Radiations. Surrey University Press, 1983.
