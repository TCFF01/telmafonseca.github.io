Radiobiology

Course Code: ENU883
Workload / Credits: 60 hours / 4 Credits
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

1. Physics & Chemistry of Radiation

  • Radiation physics and chemistry fundamental concepts; chromosomal aberrations, DNA single- and double-strand breaks.

2. Cell Survival & Dose-Response

  • Cell survival curves and mathematical models; dose-response relationships for normal tissues; tumor modeling systems.

3. Radiosensitivity & Cell Cycle

  • Radiosensitivity, mitotic cycle dynamics, radiation damage repair, dose-rate effects, oxygen effect, and tissue reoxygenation dynamics.

4. LET & Radiobiological Modifiers

  • Linear Energy Transfer (LET) and Relative Biological Effectiveness (RBE); radiosensitizing and radioprotective agents; tumor, tissue, and cell kinetics.

5. Fractionation & Radiotherapy Modalities

  • Time, dose, and fractionation relationships in clinical radiotherapy; emerging radiotherapy modalities; hyperthermia applications.

6. Radiation Effects & Molecular Radiobiology

  • Acute radiation effects, radiation carcinogenesis, hereditary radiation effects, radiation effects on embryos/fetuses, and molecular techniques in radiobiology.

  • Bernier, Donald R., Christian, Paul E., Langan, James K. (Eds.). Nuclear Medicine: Technology and Techniques. 4th Edition, Mosby Editora, 1994.
  • Perez, Carlos & Brady, Luther W. Principles and Practice of Radiation Oncology. 3rd Edition, Lippincott-Raven, 1997.
