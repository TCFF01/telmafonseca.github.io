Applied Radiochemistry

Course Code: ENU882
Program: Postgraduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology (PCTN / DEN - UFMG)
Schedule: Class Schedule (PDF)
Professor: Prof. Telma C. F. Fonseca, PhD

Course Syllabus & Main Topics

  • Atomic Structure & Chemical Bonding: Electronic structure of the atom; types and characteristics of chemical bonds.
  • Nuclear Structure & Decay: Nuclear structure and radioactive decay processes.
  • Radiation Measurement: Instrumentation for radiation detection and measurement.
  • Radionuclide Production: Production of radionuclides and radionuclide generator systems.
  • Radiopharmaceuticals: Methods of radiopharmaceutical labeling, characteristics of specific clinical radiopharmaceuticals, and quality control.
  • Nuclear Medicine & Diagnostics: Nuclear medicine aspects and the use of radiopharmaceuticals in diagnostic procedures.
  • Radiation Protection for Unsealed Sources: Safety protocols, radiation protection, and regulatory standards for handling unsealed radioactive sources.

Recommended Bibliography

  • Saha, Gopal B. Fundamentals of Nuclear Pharmacy. 4th Edition, 1997.
  • Hesselwood, S. Drug Interactions with Radiopharmaceuticals. 1994.
  • Friedlander, G. Nuclear and Radiochemistry. 3rd Edition, NY, 1981.
