Intro

Associate Professor at the Department of Nuclear Engineering (DEN), Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG). She teaches in the Graduate Program in Nuclear Science and Techniques (PCTN/UFMG), in the field of Radiation Sciences, and also collaborates with the Graduate Program in Physics at UFMG and with the Centro de Desenvolvimento da Tecnologia Nuclear (CDTN/CNEN). She is an Editorial Board Member of the international journal Applied Radiation and Isotopes (ARI) and Secretary of the Management, Diversity and Inclusion Committee of WinBrasil – Women in Nuclear, for the 2024–2027 term. She is also the founder and coordinator of the Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group (MCMEG).

She holds a degree in the computational sciences field, a Master's degree in Nuclear Science and Techniques, in the field of Radiation Sciences, from UFMG, and a Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences from the Faculty of Medicine at the Katholieke Universiteit Leuven (KU Leuven), Belgium. Between 2008 and 2014, she was a collaborating researcher at the Studiecentrum voor Kernenergie (SCK-CEN), in Mol, Belgium, where she developed her doctoral project and took part in several research projects in collaboration with French institutions.

Between 2014 and 2017, she worked as a postdoctoral researcher and head of the Internal Dosimetry Laboratory (LDI) of the Health-Applied Radiation Service at CDTN/CNEN, in Belo Horizonte (MG). She was also a lecturer and collaborator in the Graduate Program in Radiation Science and Technology, Minerals and Materials at CDTN/CNEN. In 2015, she received the International PROCORAD Award, granted by a French research group in the field of radiotoxicology, in recognition of her doctoral thesis.

She has broad experience in the fields of Computing and Nuclear Engineering, with a strong focus on computational modeling and simulation, applied mathematical methods, GPU-accelerated high-performance computing, artificial intelligence, nuclear instrumentation, and technological development in micro-, nano- and macroscopic dosimetry. Her scientific career spans research in radiotherapy, brachytherapy, nuclear medicine, radiobiology, radiopharmacy, radiological protection, neutron activation, and the development of innovative detectors and dosimetric systems.

She coordinates and supervises multidisciplinary projects in Medical Physics, internal and external dosimetry, clinical and experimental protocols, translational applications of nanoparticles, Artificial Intelligence, the development of advanced computational models, and technological solutions for research, innovation, and the training of highly qualified human resources.

MCMEG

The Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group (MCMEG) is a collaborative research initiative focused on the application of Monte Carlo methods in radiation protection, radiobiology, and dosimetry.

Through computational modeling and advanced simulation techniques, the group contributes to the development of new methodologies and tools for internal dosimetry, radiotherapy, and nanodosimetry.

Learn more at the MCMEG website.
