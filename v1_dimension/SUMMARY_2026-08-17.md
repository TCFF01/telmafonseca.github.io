## 2. Detailed Course Syllabi & Content (in English)
Full academic syllabi, course codes, workloads, class schedule links, and recommended bibliographies were translated into English and populated across all discipline pages:

- **Radiobiology (`ENU883`):** Physics & chemistry of radiation, cell survival curves, mitotic cycle, LET/RBE, fractionation, acute effects, and molecular techniques. Added IAEA TCS-42 PDF handbook resource link.
- **Radiation Applied to Biomedicine (`ENU887`):** Biological actions of radiation, radioisotope therapy (radioiodine, synovectomy, radioimmunotherapy), HDR/LDR brachytherapy, teletherapy, BNCT, and charged particle therapy.
- **Teaching Internship (`Estágio Docente`):** Instructional planning, lesson preparation, supervised teaching practice, student mentoring, and critical reflection on university teaching.
- **Applied Radiochemistry (`ENU882`):** Atomic structure, radioactive decay, radionuclide production, radiopharmaceutical labeling, quality control, and unsealed source protection.
- **Computational Methods (`ENU874`):** Analytical/variational methods, neutral particle transport, monoenergetic/multigroup finite difference, and 2D/3D finite element formulations. Includes link to MCMEG free YouTube Monte Carlo course playlist.
- **Medical Imaging (`ENU860`):** Image reconstruction from projections, X-ray transmission CT, SPECT, PET, ultrasound, tissue impedance, and MRI.
- **Nuclear Instrumentation (`ENU840`):** Radiation sources, counting statistics, ionization chambers, GM counters, scintillation spectrometry, semiconductor detectors, neutron detection, and MCA signal processing.
- **Particle Accelerators (`ENU856`):** Accelerator dynamics, Betatrons, Microtrons, Cyclotrons, Synchrotrons, LINACs, microwave power systems, nuclear codes, and clinical applications.
- **Radiation Protection (`ENU827`):** Radiation interaction, exposure/dose calculations, microdosimetry, biological risk limits, shielding calculations, and regulatory legislation.
